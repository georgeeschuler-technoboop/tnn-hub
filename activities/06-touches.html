The work

Actors against conditions

Activity 6 lays the actors against the conditions. Activity 5 mapped who is in the ecosystem. This step asks a sharper question: of all those actors, who actually touches each condition that sustains a component, and how. Where many actors converge on one condition, influence concentrates. Where a condition has almost no one attending to it, the gap is the finding.

The network report holds that reading. It traces the ties between actors and the conditions they affect, so the structure of influence across the ecosystem becomes something you can look at directly rather than infer.
